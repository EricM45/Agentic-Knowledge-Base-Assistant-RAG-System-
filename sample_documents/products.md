# Helios products

Helios sells home energy hardware and a monitoring service. Hardware quotes are project-specific. A commercial canopy block starts at 310000 dollars before structural work.

## Arc inverter

The Helios Arc hybrid inverter is sold in two sizes, 7.6 kW and 11.4 kW. Peak efficiency of the Helios Arc hybrid inverter is 98.2 percent. The standard warranty on the Helios Arc hybrid inverter is 12 years. Owners can extend that warranty to 25 years when they buy the extension before the 12th anniversary of installation. The Arc warranty covers the inverter electronics and the sealed cooling assembly. The Arc warranty does not cover damage from a missing equipment ground or from an array wired above the published voltage limit.

## Lumen battery

The Helios Lumen home battery stores 13.5 kWh and can deliver 5 kW of continuous power. Lumen cells are lithium iron phosphate. Lumen coverage lasts 10 years or until stored energy falls below 70 percent of the original capacity, whichever comes first.

## Gridline monitoring

Helios Gridline monitoring costs 8 dollars per month, or 72 dollars when the year is paid up front. Gridline records production and home-load data every 15 minutes and sends an alert when the site stops reporting.

## Field canopies

Helios Field is the commercial canopy, ordered in 250 kW blocks. A Field project needs a structural review before the permit package is submitted.
