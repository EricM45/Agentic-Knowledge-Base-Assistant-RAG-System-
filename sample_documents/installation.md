# Installation

These steps apply to residential Arc inverters and Lumen batteries.

## Site survey

After a customer signs a proposal, Helios schedules the site survey within 5 business days. The survey confirms roof condition, electrical panel space, and a path for the conduit.

## Permits

Permit review usually takes 2 to 6 weeks, and the local building department sets the pace. Helios files the permit package after the survey notes are complete.

## Install day

A standard residential installation is finished in 1 day. Commissioning takes about 2 hours and includes a Gridline connectivity test plus a walkthrough with the customer.
