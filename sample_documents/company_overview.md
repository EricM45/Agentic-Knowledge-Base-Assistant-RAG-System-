# Helios Labs

Helios Labs is a fictional sample company used as the knowledge base for this chatbot. The pages that follow describe made-up products and workplace rules so questions can be answered from local files.

## History

Helios Labs was founded on March 14, 2016 in Austin, Texas. Amina Shah is the chief executive of Helios Labs and has held that role since 2019. The company employs 420 people across offices in Austin, Denver, and Lisbon.

## Customers

Helios serves about 180,000 residential customers and 640 commercial sites. Sales cover 25 states. Independent crews install the hardware, and Helios works with 12 regional installation partners.

## Community and training

Helios hosts 16 community workshops a year for neighbors who want to learn about home energy. Field crews complete 18 hours of safety training in their first month. The Austin headquarters also runs a public open house one day each month.
