# Employee policies

These rules apply to full-time Helios employees.

## Paid time off

A new employee receives 18 days of paid time off during each of the first two years of employment. Employees in years 3 through 5 receive 23 days of paid time off per year. Employees with more than 5 years of service receive 28 days of paid time off per year. Unused paid time off up to 5 days can move into the next year.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave after a birth, an adoption, or a foster placement. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of paid parental leave for the same events. Leave can start up to two weeks before the expected date.

## Workplace

Staff based in Austin and in Denver work from the office three days a week. Lisbon staff may work remotely every workday. The home-office equipment stipend is 1500 dollars and renews every three years. Each employee has a learning budget of 2000 dollars per year.
