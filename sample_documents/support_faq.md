# Support FAQ

## Error E17

Error E17 on a Helios Arc inverter means the unit detected a DC ground fault. Turn the DC disconnect off, wait five minutes, and inspect the array wiring for a damaged connector. If error E17 returns after the disconnect is switched back on, stop using the array and write to support@helioslabs.example.

## Cold weather

The Helios Lumen battery will not charge when the cell temperature is below 32 degrees Fahrenheit, because thermal protection stays on. Charging resumes after the cells warm up on their own. Do not use an external heater unless Helios support approves it.

## Gridline sign-in

Customers sign in to Helios Gridline at https://app.helioslabs.example. Support replies within one business day. Production charts on that site refresh every 15 minutes.
